# Exceptional Review Procedure

> Synthetic training policy. This procedure creates no real entitlement or payment authority.

## 1.0 Purpose

Exceptional review addresses unusual circumstances that cannot be resolved through standard deterministic rules. It is not a method for bypassing time, mileage, evidence, authorization, or exclusion requirements.

## 2.0 Eligible referral reasons

A case may be referred when:

- Two active policies conflict or leave a material gap.
- Required evidence was destroyed by an event outside the submitting party’s control.
- A system outage prevented timely submission.
- A novel technical condition requires specialist interpretation.
- A business goodwill request is clearly separated from warranty eligibility.

## 3.0 Referral package

The package must state the standard-rule result, requested exception, business rationale, financial exposure, supporting evidence, policy citations, and prior approvals. The requesting party must not represent a pending exception as approved.

## 4.0 Authority

Only a designated human authority may approve an exception. The agent may assemble the package, identify missing information, and route the request. It may not grant the exception or alter the system of record.

## 5.0 Audit record

The final record must contain the decision, scope, authorized person, rationale, date, expiration if applicable, and affected claim IDs. An exception applies only to the documented case unless a formal policy revision states otherwise.
