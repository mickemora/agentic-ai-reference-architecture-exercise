# Parts Replacement Requirements

> Synthetic training policy. All part-handling requirements are fictional.

## 1.0 Replacement standard

A covered component should be repaired when an approved repair procedure restores intended function. Replacement is appropriate when repair is unavailable, prohibited by a technical procedure, or not economically reasonable under the fictional program standard.

## 2.0 Approved parts

The repair record must identify each installed part number and quantity. Substituted, remanufactured, or equivalent parts may be used only when permitted by the active repair procedure. Upgrades requested for preference rather than technical necessity are outside the covered scope.

## 3.0 Related damage

Related components may be included when diagnostic evidence shows they were damaged by the same covered failure. Convenient replacement, preventive replacement, and damage unrelated to the verified failure require separate justification.

## 4.0 Core and returned parts

Parts identified for return must be retained, labeled, protected from further damage, and handled under `SYN-POL-019`. Failure to return a requested part may suspend evaluation but does not automatically establish that the original failure was excluded.

## 5.0 Documentation

The claim must record the failed part, observed condition, diagnostic conclusion, repair or replacement rationale, installed part, and disposition of the removed part. Serial numbers or calibration identifiers must be included when required by the applicable component policy.
