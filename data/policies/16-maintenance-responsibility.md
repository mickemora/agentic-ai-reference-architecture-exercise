# Maintenance Responsibility and Evidence

> Synthetic training policy. Maintenance intervals and obligations are fictional.

## 1.0 General responsibility

The vehicle operator is responsible for completing maintenance appropriate to the fictional maintenance schedule and operating conditions. Maintenance services themselves are not covered repairs unless another active policy explicitly states otherwise.

## 2.0 Causal requirement

Missing maintenance records do not automatically establish that a repair is excluded. An exclusion requires evidence that required maintenance was not performed and that the omission caused or materially contributed to the failure.

## 3.0 Acceptable evidence

Evidence may include repair-facility records, itemized receipts, dated service logs, approved connected-vehicle records, or other credible documentation identifying the service, date, mileage, and materials used. A record need not originate from a participating facility to be considered.

## 4.0 Severe operating conditions

When a shorter fictional maintenance interval applies because of severe operating conditions, the claim record must identify the applicable condition and policy source. The system may not assume severe use based solely on location, occupation, or vehicle appearance.

## 5.0 Uncertain maintenance history

If records are incomplete and causality cannot be determined, the claim requires human review. The reviewer may request additional documentation or technical inspection. The system must not fabricate a maintenance history or treat absence of data as proof of neglect.

## 6.0 Privacy

Only information necessary to establish the maintenance event should be retained. Payment-account details and unrelated personal purchases must be removed or masked.
