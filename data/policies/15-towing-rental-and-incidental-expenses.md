# Towing, Rental, and Incidental Expenses

> Synthetic training policy. Amounts and reimbursement rules are fictional.

## 1.0 Towing

Reasonable towing to the nearest participating repair facility may be considered when a potentially covered failure made the vehicle unsafe or unable to operate. The record must include the tow invoice, origin, destination, date, and relationship to the repair.

## 2.0 Temporary transportation

Rental or substitute transportation may be considered only for the period when a covered repair prevents vehicle use. Delays caused solely by customer scheduling, unrelated work, or optional upgrades are excluded. Daily and total limits are determined by the fictional authorization schedule and require itemized receipts.

## 3.0 Incidental expenses

Meals, lodging, lost wages, fuel, personal property, and inconvenience are not included under the standard program. An exceptional request must follow `SYN-POL-010` and remain separate from the warranty-eligibility decision.

## 4.0 Documentation

Expense requests must identify the associated claim, dates, amounts, provider, reason, and evidence of payment. Estimates and credit-card authorization holds are not proof of final expense.

## 5.0 Decision separation

Approval of the underlying repair does not automatically approve an expense, and denial of an incidental expense does not change the repair’s technical eligibility. Each decision must retain its own evidence and authority.
