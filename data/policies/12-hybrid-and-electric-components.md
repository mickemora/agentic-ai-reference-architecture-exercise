# Hybrid and Electric Component Coverage

> Synthetic training policy. It does not describe any real electrified-vehicle warranty.

## 1.0 Scope

This policy covers evaluation requirements for fictional high-voltage traction batteries, inverters, converters, traction motors, onboard chargers, and dedicated high-voltage control modules.

## 2.0 Safety prerequisite

Diagnosis and handling must be performed by appropriately trained personnel using approved isolation and personal-protection procedures. A claim must not encourage or describe unsafe high-voltage work. Missing safety documentation suspends evaluation.

## 3.0 Traction battery evaluation

Normal capacity reduction over time is not itself a covered defect. A battery claim requires diagnostic codes, state-of-health data, cell or module imbalance results, isolation measurements when applicable, and evidence that the failure is outside the fictional service specification.

## 4.0 Charging concerns

The diagnosis must distinguish the vehicle, charging equipment, electrical supply, cable, and user configuration. External charging infrastructure is outside this synthetic program unless a separate active policy states otherwise.

## 5.0 Data minimization

Only technical information necessary for the claim may be retained. Precise travel history, personal charging habits, and location history must not be included unless explicitly required and authorized for a documented diagnostic purpose.

## 6.0 Review routing

Isolation faults, thermal events, repeated high-voltage repairs, uncertain causality, and requested complete battery replacement require specialist human review.
