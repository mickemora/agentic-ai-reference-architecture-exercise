# Basic Warranty Coverage

> Synthetic training policy. This document is not associated with any real manufacturer or warranty program.

## 1.0 Purpose

This policy defines the baseline requirements for evaluating whether a repair may qualify for coverage under the fictional training warranty program. It establishes eligibility criteria only; it does not authorize payment or replace a completed claim review.

## 2.0 General eligibility

A repair is potentially eligible when all of the following conditions are satisfied:

1. The vehicle is within the applicable time and mileage limits.
2. The failed component is included in an active coverage category.
3. The failure resulted from a defect in synthetic materials or workmanship.
4. Required diagnostic and repair documentation is complete.
5. No applicable exclusion or disqualifying modification caused the failure.

Potential eligibility means the claim may proceed to evaluation. It does not guarantee approval.

## 3.0 Covered repair scope

An eligible repair may include reasonable diagnosis, removal, replacement or repair of the covered component, and required reassembly. Labor time must be supported by the fictional labor-time standard in effect on the repair date. Unrelated maintenance, cosmetic work, upgrades, and customer-requested enhancements are outside the covered repair scope.

## 4.0 Claim decision boundary

The decision engine must use verified claim facts and the policy version effective on the repair date. Missing or conflicting evidence must route to human review. A language model, retrieval result, or service representative may explain policy but may not independently approve or deny a claim.
