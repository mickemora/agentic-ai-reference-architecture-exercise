# Authorized Repair Facility Submission Procedure

> Synthetic training policy. “Repair facility” refers only to a fictional participant.

## 1.0 Submission timing

The repair facility should submit a claim within 30 calendar days after the repair order closes. A late submission requires a documented reason and human review. Submission timing does not change the repair-date eligibility calculation.

## 2.0 Pre-submission validation

Before submission, the facility must verify:

- Claim ID, synthetic VIN, repair dates, and mileage.
- Customer concern, diagnosis, causal finding, and corrective action.
- Part numbers, quantities, labor operations, and requested amounts.
- Required photographs, measurements, authorizations, and attachments.
- Returned-part indicators and shipping instructions.

## 3.0 Duplicate prevention

The facility must search for an existing claim associated with the same repair order, component, and repair date. Suspected duplicates must be held for review rather than submitted again with a different claim identifier.

## 4.0 Corrections

Material corrections must preserve the original value, revised value, editor identity, timestamp, and reason. A correction may not be used to conceal an eligibility conflict or substitute evidence created after the repair without disclosure.

## 5.0 System acknowledgement

Receipt confirmation means the submission entered the workflow; it is not an approval. The facility remains responsible for responding to evidence requests and retaining required records.
