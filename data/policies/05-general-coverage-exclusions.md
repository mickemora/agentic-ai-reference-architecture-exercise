# General Coverage Exclusions

> Synthetic training policy. The exclusions below are fictional and intended for retrieval testing.

## 1.0 Excluded conditions

The basic program does not cover conditions caused by:

- Normal wear, gradual deterioration, or consumed maintenance items.
- Collision, vandalism, theft, fire, flooding, or external environmental events.
- Misuse, competition, overloading, or operation outside fictional published limits.
- Lack of required maintenance when a supported causal relationship exists.
- Improper prior repair or installation.
- A causally related aftermarket modification.
- Use of contaminated or incorrect fluids, fuel, or consumables.

## 2.0 Evidence requirement

An exclusion must be supported by claim-specific evidence. A category label or general suspicion is insufficient. The claim record must identify the observed condition, relevant test results, and the connection between the excluded cause and the requested repair.

## 3.0 Mixed-cause failures

If covered and excluded causes may both have contributed and the available evidence cannot separate them, the claim requires human review. The automated system must not allocate responsibility or cost without an approved deterministic rule.

## 4.0 Maintenance items

Routine replacement of filters, fluids, friction materials, bulbs, wiper elements, and similar service items is excluded unless another active policy explicitly identifies a covered defect.

## 5.0 Decision boundary

The absence of coverage evidence is not automatically proof of an exclusion. When neither eligibility nor an exclusion can be substantiated, the correct result is insufficient evidence or human review.
