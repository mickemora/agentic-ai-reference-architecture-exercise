# Powertrain Component Coverage

> Synthetic training policy. Component lists and limits are fictional.

## 1.0 Scope

This policy applies to the fictional internal-combustion engine, transmission, transfer assembly, and internally lubricated components specifically listed in Section 2.0. It does not apply to hybrid traction components governed by `SYN-POL-012`.

## 2.0 Included components

Included components are the engine block and cylinder head, internally lubricated engine parts, oil pump, water pump, transmission case, internally lubricated transmission parts, torque converter, and approved control modules dedicated to those assemblies.

External hoses, belts, filters, mounts, fluids, tune-up items, and maintenance services are not included unless their replacement is technically required as part of an otherwise covered repair.

## 3.0 Eligibility

The component must have failed because of a verified defect rather than wear, contamination, overheating caused by continued operation, improper maintenance, collision, or unauthorized calibration. The claim must include diagnostic evidence appropriate to the failure, such as pressure readings, fluid condition, fault codes, or measured internal damage.

## 4.0 Overheat and lubrication conditions

Evidence of low lubricant level or overheating requires causal analysis. The presence of discoloration or a low-fluid code alone is insufficient. The reviewer must determine whether the condition caused the failure, resulted from the failure, or is unrelated.

## 5.0 Related policy

Standard time and mileage rules in `SYN-POL-002` apply unless a future active policy explicitly defines a different powertrain term.
