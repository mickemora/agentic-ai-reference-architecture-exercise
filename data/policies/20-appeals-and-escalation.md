# Appeals and Escalation Procedure

> Synthetic training policy. This procedure is not a real dispute-resolution process.

## 1.0 Right to request review

An authorized submitting party may request review of a completed synthetic recommendation when it identifies new evidence, a factual error, an incorrect policy version, or a documented policy-interpretation concern.

## 2.0 Appeal contents

The request must include the claim ID, disputed recommendation, specific issue, supporting evidence, relevant policy citations, requested resolution, and submitter identity. Repeating the original submission without identifying a reviewable issue is insufficient.

## 3.0 Submission period

An appeal should be submitted within 30 calendar days of the recorded recommendation. A late request requires an explanation and acceptance by an authorized human reviewer.

## 4.0 Independent review

When practical, a reviewer who did not make the prior recommendation should evaluate the appeal. The reviewer receives the complete audit record, including prior evidence, tool traces, retrieved policy, and subsequent corrections.

## 5.0 Permitted outcomes

The reviewer may uphold the recommendation, revise it under authorized rules, request additional evidence, or escalate for exceptional review. The rationale and citations must be recorded.

## 6.0 Agent boundary

An agent may summarize the dispute, retrieve cited policy, identify missing evidence, and route the request. It may not overturn a recommendation, change a claim status, or represent an appeal as resolved before an authorized human decision.
